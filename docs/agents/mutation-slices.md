# Mutation testing: slice findings

One entry per rotating-slice run with a MISSED mutant (see `docs/agents/mutation-testing.md`).

- **Slice of run 36320880236 (2026-09-27), `src/watch.rs` 1110–1286:** 28 mutants, 17 caught, 8 missed, 3 unviable. Six were in `report_orphaned_prs` and `report_reconcile_failure`, which printed straight to stdout, so no unit test could see their output. They moved to `src/watch/report.rs` and now take the writer. Tests read the printed lines and kill each miss: the skipped-PR filter, the "PR"/"PRs" plural, the next-unmerged segment (a deleted `!` named an already-merged PR in the recovery hint), and the three warning-kind filters, which now share one closure. The other two were `&&`→`||` and a deleted `!` in `run_submit_phase`'s early return, which let watch skip a push it owed. `submit_phase_pushes_an_unsynced_bookmark` kills both. Its segment has to carry a non-empty change, or planning skips the segment and the test passes vacuously. All eight were hand-applied and seen to fail. The old note below, that reporting misses are often best judged equivalent, was wrong here: every one was a wrong message, and the writer seam made each a short test. One oddity: the slice reported `replace report_reconcile_failure with ()` as *caught*, although no test asserted its output, and locally emptying the printer left the full suite green. Treat a caught function-level mutant on a stdout-only function as possibly spurious.
